This is the Flagon handbook. It's the operating manual for the company: how we
work, what we value, how we pay people, how we decide things. It's public
because we think it should be, and it's the honest version because a dishonest
handbook is worse than none.

If you work here, this is the source of truth. If you're thinking about working
here, this tells you what you'd actually be signing up for. If you're just
curious how a small software company runs itself in the open, welcome. Pull up
a chair.

## Why it's public

Most companies keep the handbook internal and the marketing external, and the
two don't always agree. We collapsed that. There's no second, secret version of
how Flagon works. What you read here is what we do.

Working in the open keeps us honest. It's a lot harder to quietly adopt a
policy you'd be embarrassed to publish. If a rule can't survive being read by
the people it applies to, and by everyone else, it's probably a bad rule.

<Callout type="brand" title="The test">
  Before anything becomes "how we do things," we ask: would we be comfortable
  writing it down here, with our name on it? If not, we don't do it.
</Callout>

## How to use it

- **It's a living document.** It changes as we learn. Every page lives in
  [git](https://github.com/flagon-io), so you can see exactly what changed, when,
  and why.
- **It's not law.** It's our current best thinking. If a page is wrong, or
  reality has moved on, the page is the bug, so fix it or tell us.
- **Read what you need.** Nobody reads a handbook front to back. Start with
  [Why Flagon exists](/handbook/why-flagon-exists) and
  [How we work](/handbook/how-we-work), then come back when you have a specific
  question.

## A note on the tone

We write plainly. We'd rather be clear than impressive. If you catch us hiding
behind corporate fog ("leveraging synergies," that sort of thing), call it out.
The handbook should read like a person wrote it, because one did.
