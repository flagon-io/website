Everything Flagon makes is in the open, and the brand is no exception. The mark, the palette tokens, and the type choices all live in the source for this site, so the canonical version of the identity is never a stale file in a shared drive: it is whatever is in the repo right now. See [open source](/handbook/open-source) for where that is.

## The mark, canonically

The source of truth for the mark is the `FlagonMark` component, not an exported image. It renders crisp at any size and follows the current theme, which is why the site never ships a blurry logo. When you need a static file, export from the component in the treatment you need:

- **Full** for screen, where a gradient and sheen are welcome.
- **Flat** for print, embroidery, silkscreen, and anywhere a single spot colour is cleaner.
- **Mono** for tiny or inline use, and anywhere the mark needs to take one ink from its surroundings.

See the [visual identity](/handbook/brand-visual-identity) page for all three side by side.

## Formats

- **SVG** is the default for anything digital. It stays sharp and stays small.
- **PNG** at 2x or 3x when a raster is unavoidable, on a transparent ground.
- **Monochrome SVG** for single-colour contexts, using the mono treatment.

If you are producing something physical or high-stakes (an event backdrop, printed swag, a partner placement) and want a second pair of eyes, ask in the Discord before it goes to print. It is cheaper than a reprint.

## Clear space and minimum size

- **Clear space:** keep space around the mark equal to at least the height of the lid on every side. Do not crowd it with text or other logos.
- **Minimum size:** 16px tall on screen, a little larger in print. Below that the sheen drops away by design and the silhouette still reads.
- **Backgrounds:** the mark wants contrast. Put the full or flat treatment on a plain ground; use the mono treatment when the background is busy or a fixed colour.

## Don't

The four rules on [visual identity](/handbook/brand-visual-identity#using-the-mark) are the short version. The ones people trip on most: never ship the hollow outline (the brew is the mark), and never stretch, rotate, recolour half of it, or add effects. If you find yourself modifying the vessel, stop and use one of the three treatments instead.
